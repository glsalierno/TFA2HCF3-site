Molecular Gallery

Interactive 3D visualizations of key structures from the TFA₂HCF₃ study. Explore reaction intermediates, solvation shells, and hydrogen‑bond networks directly in your browser.

📌 About these viewers

PyMOL / VMD / OVITO are great for desktop ray-tracing but are not available in this shareable snapshot and cannot embed as rotatable widgets on GitHub. These pages use the same WebGL stick+sphere style as the PES molecule explorer.
